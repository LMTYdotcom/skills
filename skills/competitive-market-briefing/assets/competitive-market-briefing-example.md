## What mattered

### 1. Northwind - agent automation moved from add-on to standard on all paid tiers

- **Record:** Northwind's pricing page now lists its "Flows AI" agent, previously a Business-tier add-on, on all three paid plans. Seat prices are unchanged.
- **Why it may matter:** it removes the "their AI costs extra" contrast Acme has used, and may set a buyer expectation that agent automation is included rather than upsold.
- **Date / evidence:** Northwind's changelog dates the change 2026-09-09 (happened on); LMTY recorded it on 2026-09-10; still in place as of 2026-09-15. [source](https://northwind.example/changelog)
- **Continuity:** First reported here, not in the briefing of 2026-09-08

### 2. Vantage - entry price up 16.7%

- **Record:** Vantage Starter rose from $12 to $14 per seat, on both the monthly and annual list price (a 16.7% increase).
- **Why it may matter:** it narrows the gap to Acme's $15 Starter and weakens Vantage's cheapest-to-start line.
- **Date / evidence:** Vantage pricing page, seen on 2026-09-11; no date of its own on record, so the change may be older. Still in place as of 2026-09-15. [source](https://vantage.example/pricing)
- **Continuity:** First reported here, not in the briefing of 2026-09-08

### 3. Orbit - trust page states a SOC 2 Type II report

- **Record:** Orbit's new trust page states that it holds a SOC 2 Type II report. (SOC 2 is an attestation report, not a certification.) No such page was on record before 2026-09-09.
- **Why it may matter:** Orbit now states the same kind of published security assurance the other tracked competitors already state. Whether that changes any outcome depends on what buyers in active deals require, which this briefing cannot see.
- **Date / evidence:** Orbit trust page, seen on 2026-09-09. [source](https://orbit.example/trust)
- **Continuity:** Confirmed - the 2026-09-08 briefing had an Orbit security hire on its watchlist

## Pattern worth noticing

Two of six competitors have now moved agent automation from a paid add-on to included: Cardinal (its changelog dates the change 2026-08-12) and Northwind (2026-09-09). A third doing the same would strengthen this into a clear direction. It is not yet a requirement Acme has to meet; calling it that would need buyer evidence, deals where "AI included" is actually raised, which this briefing does not have. Treat as an early pattern, not a trend.

## Watchlist

- Whether a third competitor bundles agent automation into base tiers. Two of six have. Continuing from 2026-09-08.
- Whether Vantage's price increase holds or reverses. Seen on 2026-09-11.
- Whether Orbit's stated SOC 2 Type II report addresses the assurance requirements raised in Acme's active enterprise evaluations.

## Everything else
