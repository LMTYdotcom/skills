# Market Briefing

**Home product:** Acme Inc., workflow automation for operations teams

**Period:** 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-14 inclusive (7 days)

**As of:** 2026-09-15

**Coverage:** 6 tracked competitors, full period, no gaps

## What mattered

### 1. Northwind - agent automation moved from add-on to standard on all paid tiers

- **Observed:** Northwind's "Flows AI" agent, previously a Business-tier add-on, is now included on all three paid plans. Seat prices unchanged.
- **Why it may matter:** it removes the "their AI costs extra" contrast Acme has used, and sets a buyer expectation that agent automation is included rather than upsold.
- **Date / evidence:** Northwind pricing page and changelog, observed 2026-09-10, still in place as of 2026-09-15. [source](https://northwind.example/pricing)
- **Continuity:** New

### 2. Vantage - entry price up 16.7%

- **Observed:** Vantage Starter rose from $12 to $14 per seat, on both the monthly and annual list price (a 16.7% increase).
- **Why it may matter:** it narrows the gap to Acme's $15 Starter and weakens Vantage's cheapest-to-start line.
- **Date / evidence:** Vantage pricing page, observed 2026-09-11, still in place as of 2026-09-15 (held 4 days). [source](https://vantage.example/pricing)
- **Continuity:** New

### 3. Orbit - published a SOC 2 Type II report and a trust page

- **Observed:** Orbit published a SOC 2 Type II report and added a dedicated trust page. (SOC 2 is an attestation report, not a certification.)
- **Why it may matter:** Orbit now shows the same kind of published security assurance the other tracked competitors already do, closing a gap it previously had. Whether that changes any outcome depends on what buyers in active deals actually require, which this briefing cannot see.
- **Date / evidence:** Orbit trust page, observed 2026-09-09. [source](https://orbit.example/trust)
- **Continuity:** New

## Pattern worth noticing

Two of six competitors have now moved agent automation from a paid add-on to included: Cardinal last month, Northwind this week. A third competitor doing the same would strengthen this into a clear direction. It is not yet a requirement Acme has to meet; calling it that would need buyer evidence, deals where "AI included" is actually raised, which this briefing does not have. Supported by the two dated moves. Treat as an early pattern, not a trend.

## Watchlist

- Whether a third competitor bundles agent automation into base tiers. Two of six have.
- Whether Vantage's price increase holds or reverses. Four days in as of this briefing.
- Whether Orbit's published SOC 2 Type II report addresses the assurance requirements raised in Acme's active enterprise evaluations.

## Nothing else deserves attention because

Meridian only reworded its homepage heading, its third change in two months and within the same positioning, and Cardinal and Beacon had no material moves. Those are noise for this period, not signals.

## Coverage / evidence notes

All six tracked competitors were checked across the full 7-day window, most recent refresh 2026-09-15, no coverage gaps. "No material change" for Cardinal and Beacon is an observation of a quiet period, not missing coverage. The source links above point to the pages where each change was observed.
