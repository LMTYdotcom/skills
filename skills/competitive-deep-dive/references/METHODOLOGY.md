# Competitor Deep Dive Methodology

## Research as a reusable baseline

The deep dive is the skill most likely to become shared context for other skills. Structure it so facts are reusable and interpretations are easy to update.

## Recommended lenses

### Customer

- target segments
- jobs/use cases
- purchase triggers
- sales motion

### Position

- category
- promise
- value themes
- proof

### Product

- workflows
- breadth/depth
- important capabilities
- ecosystem
- operational constraints

### Monetization

- price
- package
- entitlement
- expansion

### Momentum

- recent product movement
- pricing movement
- messaging movement
- market/customer signals

### Perception

- why buyers choose them
- why buyers leave or reject them
- sentiment caveats

## Strength versus weakness language

Prefer:
- "stronger fit when..."
- "trade-off is..."
- "public evidence suggests..."

Avoid:
- "bad at"
- "weak" without evidence
- rhetorical takedowns

## New entrant protocol

For a poorly understood entrant:
- separate observed facts from unknowns
- identify evidence of actual customer traction if available
- avoid promoting a noisy entrant to strategic importance based on novelty alone
- recommend `competitive-market-shift-analysis` when threat level is the real question
