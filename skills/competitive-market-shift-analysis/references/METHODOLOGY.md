# Market Shift Analysis Methodology

## Shift model

A useful market-shift analysis combines three layers.

### Supply-side movement

What companies are building, pricing, packaging, and saying.

### Demand-side movement

What customers are asking for, switching to, paying for, and using.

### Enabling or constraining forces

Technology, economics, regulation, distribution, or ecosystem changes that make a pattern more likely to persist.

A strong shift often has evidence in all three layers.

## Signal weighting

Consider:
- directness
- recency
- independence
- magnitude
- customer relevance
- persistence

A high-profile announcement can be low-signal if it has little buyer impact. A quiet repeated objection across 30 sales calls can be high-signal.

## Trend versus fashion

Ask:
- Does the change alter economics or customer behavior?
- Does it reduce meaningful friction?
- Are customers adopting it without heavy persuasion?
- Are multiple players converging independently?
- Does the pattern persist across more than one launch cycle?

## Scenario framing

When the evidence is early, present scenarios rather than a single prediction.

Example:
- scenario A: feature becomes baseline expectation
- scenario B: feature remains a niche workflow
- scenario C: feature is absorbed into a broader platform capability

For each, identify observable indicators.
