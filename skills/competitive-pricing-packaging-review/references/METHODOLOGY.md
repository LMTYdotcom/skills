# Pricing and Packaging Review Methodology

## Three evidence layers

Keep these separate.

### Market structure

What competitors charge and how they package value.

### Buyer behavior

What customers and prospects react to in real evaluations.

### Business economics

What the home company can profitably and strategically charge.

A market scan can inform a pricing decision. It cannot replace the other two layers.

## Packaging analysis dimensions

- entry point
- upgrade path
- core value metric
- plan boundaries
- entitlement logic
- add-on strategy
- free/trial strategy
- enterprise gate
- usage or overage model
- procurement complexity
- predictability

## Market-pattern warning

Do not infer correctness from convergence. Several competitors can copy the same bad pricing pattern.

Treat convergence as evidence of customer expectation only when buyer behavior or adoption evidence supports it.

## Revenue lifecycle lens

Pricing and packaging affect:
- acquisition friction
- activation and time to value
- expansion paths
- renewal and retention
- sales efficiency

A packaging recommendation that optimizes entry but destroys expansion may be strategically worse.

## Pricing change review

For a proposed home-company change, inspect:
- affected segments
- existing-customer migration
- new-customer acquisition
- expansion mechanics
- sales compensation or discounting
- product usage and value realization
- competitor response risk
- communication and trust
