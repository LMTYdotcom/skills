# Hiring and Leadership Signals Methodology

## Why hiring gets over-read

A job ad is public, specific and dated, which makes it feel like a window into a plan. It is one team's request for one person. It may be a replacement, a speculative listing kept open all year, or a role that is filled before anyone reads it.

The discipline is to move from ads to counts, from counts to movement, and only from movement to a reading.

## What a count needs before it means anything

| Need | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The source kind | A whole board, one page and a sample are three different numbers |
| The read date | Roles turn over in weeks |
| An earlier count from the same source | Without it there is no movement, only a level |
| The company's size | Ninety open roles is routine at 8,000 people and a doubling at 150 |

When any of the four is missing, say which, and lower the claim to match.

## Signals, and what each can carry

| Seen | Consistent with | Does not show | Would confirm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open roles up sharply against 90 days ago, same source | Expansion, or a backlog of approved hires | Revenue growth; that the roles will be filled | Headcount rising over the following months; a funding or results announcement |
| Open roles down sharply | A hiring freeze, hires completed, or a board being cleaned up | Layoffs | A statement, press, or headcount falling |
| A first role in a function (first sales hire, first security lead) | The function is being started | That it exists yet | The role closing; a named person in it later |
| Several roles in one new country | Entering or building in that market | A launched local product | A local entity, a localized site, local customers |
| Roles naming a product or technology not on the site | Work on it is planned or under way | A launch date; that it will ship | Docs, a changelog entry, a beta |
| Senior go-to-market roles after a product-led history | A move toward sales-led selling | That the motion has changed | Enterprise pricing, a sales-contact route, case studies with larger buyers |
| The same role open for many months | Hard to fill, or left up on purpose | Urgency | The role closing |
| A leader leaves with no successor named | A gap in that function | Trouble | An interim named; the role posted |
| No arrival or departure on record | Nothing by itself: it says how far back the record reaches | A stable team, or any span of years without change | An earlier dated reading of the team page; press on the period |
| Several leaders hired from one company | A network effect around one hire | A deliberate strategy to raid a rival | Their roles and timing |

Use the "consistent with" column as a ceiling. If the evidence is one role, stop at "seen".

## Reading titles

Quote, then note only what the words say:

- "Account Executive, Commercial - Singapore": a sales role, a segment, a place
- "Forward Deployed Engineer": a customer-facing engineering role, which is a kind of selling motion
- "Staff Engineer, Billing": work on billing

Do not expand a title into a roadmap. The job description may say more; when you have read it, quote it and link it.

## Function mix

Report counts by function and name the largest two. Compare to an earlier mix only from the same source. With no earlier mix, describe the level: "of 92 open roles, 30 are in sales and 23 in engineering".

A mix dominated by one function is common in a company that hires in batches. Say what it is before saying what it means.

## Leadership changes

For each change: the person, the role, the date, and how it is known.

| How it is known | Write |
| --- | --- |
| The company's own page or announcement | "joined as", "left, per the company" |
| Press | "reported to have joined" |
| A role that stopped being listed | "no longer listed on the team page as of" |
| Inferred from a later role elsewhere | "now lists a role at [company]; no departure date stated" |

A change of title is not a departure. A founder moving from one office to another is still there.

With no dated arrival or departure on record, write "no arrivals or departures on record since [the date the record's leadership entries were first seen]". A team page read once cannot show who left before it was read: never "stable", "unchanged" or a span of years.

## Where people come from and go

The record covers the people a profile knows: founders, leaders, people who have appeared in public. Use it for statements like "three of its eight known leaders previously worked at Initech". Do not use it for "it hires mostly from Initech".

These rows carry no grade and no source: write "the record lists" and say they are ungraded. A signal needs the tie's grade, which is on the person's own profile: read it first, or build none.

## People

Roles, dates, employers and what someone said in public. Nothing else. If a request reaches for more, say that this skill covers the public professional record and continue with that.

## Length

A hiring read is short: the numbers, five or six quoted titles, the leadership table, and at most three signals written out in full. If it runs longer, the signals are too many to all be real.
